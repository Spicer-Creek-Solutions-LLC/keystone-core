# Code of Conduct (Minimal & Pragmatic)

Keystone Core is a technical project focused on infrastructure, correctness, and operator experience.
We welcome contributions from anyone who wants to help.

### Expectations

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus on technical arguments, not personal attacks.
- Assume good intent.
- No harassment, discrimination, or bigotry.

### Unacceptable Behavior

Behavior that makes collaboration unpleasant — including personal attacks, harassment, or ongoing
drama — may result in removal from discussions or contribution spaces.

### Enforcement

This project uses a BDFL + maintainer governance model. The BDFL or maintainers may take action to
resolve problems, including removing individuals if necessary. This should be rare.

### Why Is This So Short?

Because we prefer culture over bureaucracy and believe most contributors act in good faith. If we
ever find ourselves writing lots of rules, something has gone wrong.

If you have questions or concerns, open a discussion or contact the maintainers privately.
